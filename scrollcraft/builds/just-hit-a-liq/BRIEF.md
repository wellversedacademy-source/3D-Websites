## Grammar

**Garment study (new grammar).** One physical object held on a fixed stage for
the whole page. Sections are views of that object (front, back, detail,
layers), never new scenes. Structure:

- **Nav:** a price ladder on the right edge. Each chapter is a price level; the
  marker falls as you descend; it is real navigation (links jump).
  Conventional shop navigation stays in the top bar on every page.
- **Sequence:** every chapter must change what you see of the same garment
  (pose, light, layers). A chapter that does not change the object is cut.
- **Ending:** the mark itself, resolved at page width, with its level drawn
  across.
- **Bans:** video and scrub clips; second objects on the stage; pinned
  crossfade type acts; card rails; tilt and magnet devices; any scene change.

Why not the eight defined grammars: filmic one-shot implies a camera journey
through scenes, which is the reference's cliché and costs video; chaptered
editorial bans media under type, but the garment must sit under the copy;
live surface would mean a trading dashboard, which the owner banned; continuous
world needs geography the brand does not have; typographic poster and
rhythmic cutlist lose the object; gallery would make the homepage a shop grid;
split stage has no two-sided argument here.

## Signature move: the strike

A price ladder doubles as chapter navigation. Scrolling lowers the price. When
it crosses the cobalt level (4,370.00, the level on the hoodie board), the line
runs across the viewport and continues the cobalt silicone line on the
garment's back, which ignites, and
the page stamps `LIQUIDITY / SWEPT` with the visitor's own clock time. That
time returns on the woven edition tag in the access form ("Your strike:
21:14:07 EST") and is saved with a sign-up.

**Tell-someone sentence:** it's the site where you scroll the price down until
you take the liquidity yourself, and the hoodie's cobalt line lights up with
your timestamp.

## Feeling curve (written before the score)

| Act | Feeling | Cause on screen |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Intrigue | A dark garment under one light, the mark large, one line of copy |
| Weight | Tactility | Light rakes the hood and shoulder; construction callouts track the garment |
| The mark | Hush | Garment turns its back in near darkness; three campaign lines arrive |
| **Strike (peak)** | **Arrival** | The level is taken: cobalt line across screen and garment, time stamped |
| Construction | Curiosity, control | The back graphic lifts into its four fabrication layers; visitor switches views and colorways |
| Drop 001 | Desire | Plain, readable product cards; the page stops performing |
| Manifesto | Resolve | Low camera, garment far back in haze, four lines |
| Access | Belonging | A woven edition label carrying their strike time |
| Close | Certainty | The mark at full width, the level drawing across it |

**Peak:** "I scrolled the price down and when it hit the line the whole thing
went blue and stamped my time on it." Lives in the strike act, which has the
largest span (260svh desktop, 200svh phone) and a held, sticky composition.

**Authored silence:** the meaning act (170svh) is intentionally dim and sparse;
it is the silence before the peak, not dead scroll.

## Score

| Beat | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Fixed stage + static hero copy | The object must be compelling before any scroll |
| Weight | Camera orbit + tracked annotations (`flow`) | Construction is shown on the object, not described beside it |
| The mark | `cue` lines in a `flow` act + `drift` to near black | Copy assembles while the garment turns away |
| Strike | Sticky hold + bespoke strike (page JS on scroll) | The peak needs the most room and one change |
| Construction | Exploded decal layers + controls (agency) | Deconstruction is literal, and the visitor drives it |
| Drop 001 | `flow`, opaque, conventional cards | Shopping must be easy |
| Manifesto | `cue` lines + low camera | Slow, single idea |
| Access | Form as woven label | The signature move pays off |
| Close | `in` + level drawn on the mark | Resolves and holds |

Device families used: stage pose, annotation tracking, `cue`, `drift`, sticky
hold, bespoke strike, explode layers, `in`. Total length ≈ 14.8 viewport-heights
desktop, 13.9 phone, 9 sections.
